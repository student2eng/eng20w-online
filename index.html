Step 1 · Discover

New Words from Your Level and Lower Levels

New vocabulary is selected according to your learning level, while also bringing in useful words from levels you have already passed.

Your current level comes first. The selection aims for approximately two-thirds of new words from your current level and one-third from lower levels.

Available words from lower levels are distributed across those levels. If there are not enough words in one group, the system fills the remaining places from other available levels.

For example, a B1 learner can receive new words from B1, A2 and A1. Words from higher levels are not included.

Step 2 · Practise Again

Unmastered Words Return in Your Learning Sessions

Words you find difficult are not left behind. They return as part of your core learning words until you are ready to master them.

🔄

Revisit Difficult Words

When you choose “I Don't Know”, the word is marked as unmastered and becomes eligible for future learning sessions.

↗

Prioritise Older Words

The system gives priority to words that have remained unmastered for longer.

✓

Move Towards Mastery

When you know an unmastered word, it enters the spaced repetition review cycle.

Step 3 · Remember

Review Mastered Words at Scheduled Intervals

After you mark a word as known, English20w schedules it for review. Each successful review moves the word to its next interval.

5

After 5 Days

Your first scheduled review after mastering a new or previously unmastered word.

10

After 10 More Days

After completing the first review successfully, the next review is scheduled 10 days later.

1M

After One Month

After the second review, the next review is scheduled one month later. The cycle then starts again with a 5-day interval.

What if you do not remember a word during review? It returns to your unmastered words and can appear again in future core learning sessions.

Frequently asked questions

About English20w English Vocabulary Learning

Find out how learning sessions, vocabulary selection and spaced repetition work.

What is English20w?

English 20 words is an English vocabulary learning platform that helps learners build their vocabulary through focused learning sessions, repeated practice and spaced repetition.

How many words are included in each session?

A session can include up to 20 core learning words and up to 10 additional words due for review, for a maximum of 30 words depending on availability.

How does English20w select new vocabulary?

New words are selected from your current CEFR level and lower levels only. The selection aims for approximately two-thirds from your current level and one-third from lower levels.

What happens to words I have not mastered?

Words marked as unmastered return in future regular learning sessions. Older unmastered words are prioritised.

How does spaced repetition work?

The first review is after 5 days. After a successful first review, the next is 10 days later. After a successful second review, the next is one month later. The cycle then restarts with a 5-day interval.

Which English levels does English20w support?

English 20 words supports five CEFR levels: A1 Beginner, A2 Elementary, B1 Intermediate, B2 Upper Intermediate and C1 Advanced.
